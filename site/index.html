App Store Connect and Firebase.
One app.

A free, open-source iPhone and iPad app. Ship releases, reply to reviews, manage TestFlight, certificates and your team, right from your phone.

Everything you open the website for

Releases

Edit metadata and localizations, create a version, run a pre-submission checklist, submit for review and release, on iOS, macOS, tvOS and visionOS.

Reviews

Read customer reviews from every storefront and reply from your phone, with reusable reply templates.

TestFlight

Beta groups, testers, builds and beta review information.

Certificates and team

Certificates, provisioning profiles, identifiers and devices. Invite members and edit roles and visible apps.

Analytics

A dashboard plus daily, weekly and monthly report charts, and Home Screen widgets for what is in review.

Firebase

Projects and apps, Remote Config parameters and conditions, and Cloud Messaging with audience targeting.

Built to be verified

No server in between

The app talks directly to Apple's and Google's official APIs. There is no StackConnect backend.

Network Audit

Settings › Privacy › Network Audit lists every request the app sends and receives, with secrets masked. Export the log and check it yourself.

Open source, MIT

Read the code, build it yourself or send a fix on GitHub. Credentials stay in the iOS Keychain.
