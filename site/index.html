Questions

Can I submit an app for App Review from my iPhone?

Yes. Edit version metadata, run a pre-submission checklist, submit for App Review, and cancel, release or resubmit a version from your iPhone or iPad.

Is StackConnect free?

Yes. It is free on the App Store and open source under the MIT license.

Does it send my data to its own server?

No. The app talks directly to Apple's and Google's official APIs. Credentials stay in the iOS Keychain, and Network Audit shows every request the app makes.

Does it work with Firebase?

Yes. Browse Firebase projects and apps, edit Remote Config parameters and conditions, and send Cloud Messaging notifications.
