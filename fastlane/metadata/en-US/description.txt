StackConnect is a free, open-source companion for app developers. Manage your App Store Connect, Google Play Console and Firebase accounts from one native iPhone and iPad app.

HOME
• Every store at a glance: apps in review, awaiting release and recent reviews
• Home Screen widgets for In Review, Awaiting Release and Recent Reviews
• A reminder when the Account Holder needs to accept new agreements

APP STORE CONNECT
• Browse apps with search, sort, favorites and archived apps
• Edit version metadata, manage localizations and preview screenshots
• Manage versions on iOS, macOS, tvOS and visionOS, including phased release status
• Submit for review with a pre-submission checklist; cancel, release or resubmit
• Track and discard App Review submissions
• Edit App Information, category, age rating, privacy and accessibility declarations
• Read and reply to customer reviews, with reusable reply templates
• Analytics dashboard plus App Store Connect analytics reports with daily, weekly and monthly charts; download and manage report files
• TestFlight: beta groups, testers, builds and beta review information
• Certificates, provisioning profiles, identifiers and devices: create, inspect and import
• Team: invite members, edit roles, resources and visible apps

GOOGLE PLAY CONSOLE
• Browse your apps and their app information
• Follow releases on every track
• View store listings
• Read and reply to ratings and reviews
• Invite and remove users and manage their app permissions

FIREBASE
• Browse projects and apps
• Manage Remote Config parameters and conditions
• Send Cloud Messaging notifications with audience targeting

MULTI-ACCOUNT AND TEAMS
• Add several accounts per provider
• Granular permissions per account and per app
• Share accounts with your team through encrypted exports, scoped to specific apps
• Offline-first: cached data loads instantly and syncs in the background; an indicator shows when you are offline

PRIVACY
Credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain, and the app talks directly to the official Apple, Google and Firebase APIs — there are no StackConnect servers. Anonymous usage analytics help improve the app and can be turned off at any time in Settings.

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
StackConnect is MIT-licensed and free, with no ads and no subscriptions. If it saves you time, an optional tip helps keep it going.
